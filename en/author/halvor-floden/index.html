Free from when, country by country

  • EU (base rule) and Germany: from 1 January 2027.
  • France: from 1 January 2027. For works published before 3 September 1939 the wartime extension moves the date to about April 2035.
  • Italy: from 1 January 2027. If the 6-year extension (works published before 1945) is added to the 70 years: from 1 January 2033. For authors from Allied countries there may be a further extension.
  • Spain: not before 1 January 2037 (80 years after death, because the author died before 1987; the law does not say how the year is counted).
