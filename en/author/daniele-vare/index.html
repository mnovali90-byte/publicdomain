What a record is: the entry a library uses to describe a publication. There is usually one for each edition or reprint, so a book published five times counts five. Records are not copies sold, nor different titles.

Bibliographic records in English linked to the author in the British Library catalogue, searched by surname and years of birth and death; a record counts only if surname and years belong to the same person. Some records catalogued differently may be missed. They include every publication linked to the person, including those they contributed to (translations, editing, prefaces), not only books they wrote: it measures how present they are in the catalogue.

Questions

Short answers

When does Daniele Varè enter the public domain?

In the UK and the EU on 1 January 2027: Daniele Varè died in 1956, and the works become free 70 years after death, from 1 January of the following year. In Spain on 1 January 2037.

Who they were

Daniele Varè was an Italian expatriate diplomat and author, most famous for the China-set novel The Maker of Heavenly Trousers. He is also remembered for Laughing Diplomat, his autobiography as Italian diplomat.
