The works of Craig Rice enter the public domain in Italy, France, Germany and the UK on 1 January 2028; in Spain on 1 January 2038. In the USA it depends on each work’s publication year.

They show how much an author is already published in each country. What a record is: The entry a library uses to describe a publication. There is usually one for each edition or reprint, so a book published five times counts five. Records are not copies sold, nor different titles. They include every publication linked to the person, including those they contributed to (translations, editing, prefaces), not only books they wrote: it measures how present they are in the catalogue. In the British Library catalogue the author is searched by surname and years of birth and death; a record counts only if surname and years belong to the same person. Some records catalogued differently may be missed.
