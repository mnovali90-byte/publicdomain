Paul Zech

1881 – 1946

Died in 1946, in the European Union the works are free from 1 January 2017. Other countries have different rules, below.

Free from when, country by country

  • EU (base rule) and Germany: from 1 January 2017.
  • France: from 1 January 2017. For works published before 3 September 1939 the wartime extension moves the date to about April 2025.
  • Italy: from 1 January 2017. If the 6-year extension (works published before 1945) is added to the 70 years: from 1 January 2023. For authors from Allied countries there may be a further extension.
  • Spain: not before 1 January 2027 (80 years after death, because the author died before 1987; the law does not say how the year is counted).
