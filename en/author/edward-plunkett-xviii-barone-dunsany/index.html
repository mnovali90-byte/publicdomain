Died in 1957, in the European Union the works are free from 1 January 2028. Other countries have different rules, below.

Free from when, country by country

  • EU (base rule) and Germany: from 1 January 2028.
  • France: from 1 January 2028. For works published before 3 September 1939 the wartime extension moves the date to about April 2036.
  • Italy: from 1 January 2028. If the 6-year extension (works published before 1945) is added to the 70 years: from 1 January 2034. For authors from Allied countries there may be a further extension.
  • Spain: not before 1 January 2038 (80 years after death, because the author died before 1987; the law does not say how the year is counted).

Who they were

Edward John Moreton Drax Plunkett, 18th Baron Dunsany, commonly known as Lord Dunsany, was an Anglo-Irish writer and dramatist. He published more than 90 books during his lifetime, and his output consisted of hundreds of short stories, plays, novels, and essays; further works were published posthumously.
