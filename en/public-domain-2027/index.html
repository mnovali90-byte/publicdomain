Before you publish

What it really means

The text, not the edition

The original text is free. Recent translations, illustrations and edited editions have their own rights: to publish a translation you need a new one, or one that is already free.

It depends where you publish

On 1 January not everything expires everywhere at once. The same book can be free in the USA and protected in Europe, or the other way round. «Winnie-the-Pooh» has been free in the USA since 2022, and in Europe only from 2027.

Certain and to be checked

We mark as certain only what more than one source confirms (years of death, years of publication). The rest is «to be checked», with the reason. Sources are listed at the bottom of every page.

Questions

The short answers

What is Public Domain Day?

It is 1 January of every year, the day copyright expires for works that have reached the legal limit: in the European Union and the UK 70 years after the author's death (Directive 2006/116/EC, Art. 1), in the USA 95 years after publication (17 U.S.C. § 304).

What enters the public domain in 2027?

In Italy, France, Germany and the UK, the works of writers who died in 1956. In Spain, those of writers who died in 1946. In the USA, books published in 1931.

Why do authors who died in 1946 enter in Spain?

For authors who died before 7 December 1987, Spanish law still applies the 80 years of the 1879 law (Ley de Propiedad Intelectual, fourth transitional provision).

Is every book published in 1931 really free in the USA?

Not always. Works first published in the USA whose copyright was not renewed after 28 years were already free earlier. Foreign works that regained protection in 1996 (17 U.S.C. § 104A) last 95 years from publication. We call a book free only when a second source confirms the year it came out.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is general information built from open data, and it may contain errors. Before publishing, check the law and the facts for your country and your edition.
