Sources and rules

Wikidata (CC0) for works, authors and years; Wikipedia for this week's page views; Duke University's Public Domain Day lists (CC BY 4.0) as a second source for the USA; Wikimedia Commons for portraits, each with its own licence. Data updated automatically every month: it may contain errors.

Rules used: Italy, France, Germany, United Kingdom 70 years from the author's death (in the UK, works first published after 1988 by authors who died before 1969 are protected until 2039); Spain 80 years for authors who died before 7 December 1987; USA 95 years from first publication (the first recorded date is used). In Italy, for works published before 1940 by authors from Allied countries in the Second World War, a note recalls that some argue for an extension (1947 Peace Treaty), not applied by courts so far.

Before publishing

The dates are an indicative calculation from the rules listed on the country pages. Not legal advice: check the law of the country before publishing. They refer to the original works: translations, illustrations and recent editions have their own rights. Anyone using this data does so at their own risk; for a commercial edition, ask a copyright expert for advice.
