What becomes free, and when

Every 1 January, authors and works leave copyright. See them in advance here, country by country, with the legal rules and the books to start from, plus the year's literary anniversaries.

For publishers, translators, creators and anyone who talks about books. Open data from Wikidata, Wikipedia and SBN, official legal texts, updated automatically every month.

Who enters the public domain on 1 January 2027

Authors who died in 1956: in the European Union their works become free on 1 January 2027.

  • B. R. Ambedkar (1891–1956)

    Basic EU rule: works become free on 1 January of the year after the 70th anniversary of the author's death. Some countries, Italy included, have extensions: check national rules before publishing. An existing translation has its own rights: this site only covers original works.
