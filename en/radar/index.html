What becomes
free, and when

Every 1 January, authors and works leave copyright. See them in advance here, country by country, with the legal rules and the books to start from, plus the year's literary anniversaries.

For publishers, translators, creators and anyone who talks about books. Open data from Wikidata, Wikipedia and national catalogues (SBN, BnF, BNE, British Library and Library of Congress), official legal texts, updated automatically every three months and in December.

Who enters the public domain on 1 January 2027

Authors who died in 1956: in Italy, France, Germany and the UK their works become free on 1 January 2027.

  • Bertolt Brecht (1898–1956)
  • B. R. Ambedkar (1891–1956)
  • A. A. Milne (1882–1956)
  • Jakub Kolas (1882–1956)

        Sources

        Fonti: Wikidata (CC0), Wikipedia (testi CC BY-SA, con link alla pagina d'origine), Wikimedia Commons (ritratti, ciascuno con la sua licenza), Project Gutenberg. Aggiornato in automatico ogni tre mesi e a dicembre.

        Basic EU rule: works become free on 1 January of the year after the 70th anniversary of the author's death. Some countries have different rules (Spain 80 years for authors who died before 1987, France 30 more years for those who «died for France»): check national rules before publishing. An existing translation has its own rights: this site only covers original works.

        Before publishing

        Anyone using this data does so at their own risk. The dates are indicative and refer to the original works: translations, illustrations and recent editions have their own rights. For a commercial edition, ask a copyright expert for advice.
